The player

The player shows the song Music.app plays and lets the person control it. It has four parts in the window. This page explains each part. Playback explains how a song starts and how the queue works. The window and a frame explains how a frame draws.

The code is in Odin. It builds the whole window again each frame, as an immediate-mode UI does. The app draws a frame only when something asks for one. The frame loop explains that. The data about the playing song comes from the helper.

What the person sees

  • The player bar runs along the bottom of the window. Its left end shows the cover, the song name, a "LOSSLESS" or "YOUTUBE" badge, a heart and the artist and album. The middle holds shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat and the seek bar with two clocks. The right end holds the side panel button and the volume bar.
  • The side panel opens right of the page. Two tabs at its foot say "Up Next" and "Lyrics". Up Next lists the queue from the song playing on.
  • The lyrics follow the song word by word. The current line shows brighter than the others. Each word takes the accent colour as the song reaches it. A "Romaji" chip and a "Translation" chip show a second line under each line, when the song has one.
  • The Now Playing page opens with a click on the cover in the player bar. It shows the cover large on a blurred copy of its colours. Beside the cover come the song, its artist, its album and pills such as "Lossless" and "Dolby Atmos". Under them come the "Favourite", "Add to Playlist" and "Copy Link" buttons and the current lyric line. Below, the PLAYS, LAST PLAYED, IN LIBRARY SINCE and SKIPS cards give numbers. The CREDITS and SOUND cards and "More by" the artist come last.

The accent colour comes from the cover of the playing song. Each frame, kit.update_accent reads the cover that find_player_artwork_source finds. It keeps the result in core.app.accent.

From the helper to the sung word

Step through the diagram to follow the now-playing state from a helper post to the word the lyrics light. Click a box to read what it does.

Music.app posts its state only when it changes. to_position_seconds estimates the position between posts from position_read_at_ms. Two cases keep the last position as it is. In the first, the last position post did not move on, so stall_count holds more than 0. In the second, the song did not start yet, so has_started holds false. Music.app says it plays while it still loads a song. The clock then stays still.

A post counts as moved on when its position runs more than MIN_PROGRESS_SECONDS (0.5 s) past the last one. apply_helper_messages calls helper.apply for each message, before the frame builds its boxes.

How the lyrics load

Apple's API gives lyrics as TTML. TTML is an XML format for timed text. Each line is a <p> tag with a begin time. In a song timed by word, each word is a <span> tag with its own begin time.

lyrics.watch holds the lines of one song at a time. A new song moves the old lines to last_arena. The frame that asked for the new song may still show them. The next new song frees them. Memory explains arenas.

fetch_lyrics compares its song with the song that watch holds now. When another song started while the lines loaded, it frees the lines and stops.

The query LYRICS_QUERY asks Apple for English translations and for Latin letters. The answer then holds a second TTML in ttmlLocalizations. fetch_lines reads that one when it has text. Else it reads ttml.

Each line has a key, such as "L1". to_line_texts reads the transliteration block for the romaji and the translation block for the English. to_lines keeps the romaji only when its letters differ from the line's own letters. An English song thus shows no romaji line.

A Word holds its begin time and its UTF-16 range in the line's text. CoreText counts text in UTF-16 units. to_utf16_length counts a character above U+FFFF as two units. The space after a word stays with the word, so the words join back into the line.

The lyrics also name their songwriters. to_songwriters reads them. The CREDITS card shows them as "Lyrics by".

The player bar

build_player_bar builds three parts, left to right:

  • build_song_info builds the cover, the name, the badge, the heart and the artist and album. A click on the cover opens the Now Playing page. A click on the artist and album opens the artist page.
  • build_controls builds the five buttons and the seek bar. A right-click on shuffle opens the shuffle's rules.
  • build_player_end builds the side panel button and the volume bar.

The bar sends its commands to Music.app through the helper. The play button sends playPause. A change on the seek bar sends seek, but only while a song plays or waits paused. A change on the volume bar sends setVolume. It also sets the volume in core.app.now_playing at once.

The bar does not always show what Music.app plays.

  • When Music.app plays nothing, find_shown_song returns the last song, paused where it was. A click on play then calls resume_last_song.
  • While the queue puts a song into the library, or the YouTube page loads it, the bar shows the queue song. Its second line says "Adding to library…" or "Loading from YouTube…".
  • is_loading holds true when Music.app says Playing but the song did not start and its position stalls. The bar then says "Loading in Music.app…".
  • With no song at all, the bar says "Not playing". Its second line says "Music.app is closed" or "Nothing in Music.app".

A queue song from YouTube shows the "YOUTUBE" badge in place of "LOSSLESS". The heart calls playback.toggle_favourite.

The clock shows whole seconds. No other part asks for a frame when the second turns over. build_progress asks for a frame 5 ms after the next whole second, while a song plays.

The side panel

build_panel animates the panel's width from 0 to PANEL_WIDTH_POINTS (320 points). It builds the open tab, then the tabs at the foot. It returns true while the lyrics move. The frame then asks for the next frame.

The side panel button calls core.toggle_side_panel. It opens the tab the panel showed last, or closes the panel. A click on a tab calls core.set_panel, which saves the choice in the store as panel.

build_up_next lists the queue from the song playing on. It builds boxes only for the rows in view, plus two rows on each side. Two spacers hold the height of the other rows. Each row is 52 points high.

build_queue_row builds one row. The row of the song playing takes the accent tint. Another row shows a remove button while the mouse is on it. A click on a row plays the queue from that row. A right-click opens the song menu. A drag takes the song onto a playlist. While the queue plays shuffled, a note above the list shows the shuffle and a "Rules" button.

Lyrics timing

The lyrics run ahead of Music.app's position by the lead. DEFAULT_LEAD_MS sets it to 500 ms. Music.app's posts run about half a second behind what it plays.

Cmd ] runs the lyrics 50 ms earlier. Cmd [ runs them 50 ms later. apply_lead_key keeps the lead within 2000 ms either way. It saves the lead in the store as lyricLeadMs. start_lyrics reads it at the start. For 2 s after a change, build_lead_note shows a note such as "Lyrics 0.50 s ahead of Music.app. Cmd [ and Cmd ] change it."

Each frame, build_lyrics works out the lyrics' time from the estimate and the lead. Then:

  1. find_current_line picks the last line that began.
  2. The column slides so that the line three before the current one shows at the top. It reads the line's place from last frame's layout. It glides at SLIDE_RATE.
  3. build_lyric_line builds each line. The current line fades to full opacity. The others fade to 0.7.
  4. The text box of each line gets color_lyric_glyph as its color_of. The text drawing calls it for each glyph with the glyph's UTF-16 index.

color_lyric_glyph finds the word that holds the glyph. In the current line, a sung word fades from dim to the accent over WORD_FADE_SECONDS (0.15 s). In the line before, the sung words fade back to dim as the current line starts. The romaji line uses the same procedure with its own timed words. The translation line has no timing.

schedule_lyrics decides when the next frame comes. While a word still fades, it returns true. The frame loop then keeps drawing. Else find_next_change finds the next word's begin time. schedule_lyrics asks for a frame 5 ms after it. The app then draws no frame between words.

build_lyric_toggles shows the "Romaji" chip only when a line has romaji. It shows the "Translation" chip only when a line has a translation. Romaji shows until the person turns it off. The translation shows only after the person turns it on. The store saves both as lyricsRomaji and lyricsTranslation.

The Now Playing page

build_now_playing_page needs Apple's catalog data for the song. A queue song has its catalog ID. For any other song, find_catalog_id searches the catalog by name and artist. It takes the exact match, or else the first result. Then the page watches the song with its albums and artists. Pages and their data explains apple.watch and page data.

read_song_details reads the answer into Song_Details. Until Apple answers, the page shows what Music.app gave. From the top, the page builds:

  1. build_hero builds the large cover, the title, the artist and the album with its label. Below them come the pills, the buttons and the current lyric line. The lyric line uses the same lyrics, lead and word timer as the Lyrics tab.
  2. build_stats builds the four stat cards, only for a song in the library.
  3. build_detail_cards builds the CREDITS and SOUND cards.
  4. build_more_by shows up to six releases of the artist, newest first. A release from the last 30 days says "NEW" and its date.

Details from Music.app and from Apple

Apple's API and Music.app each know part of the song. watch_song_info asks Music.app, through the helper, at most once a minute for each song. Music.app gives the play count, the dates, the skips, the sample rate, the bit rate, the BPM and the file size. Apple gives the composer, the label, the genre, the release date, the ISRC and the audio traits. Music.app names a streamed song "HLS media". to_format then reads the format from Apple's traits.

Stats from the play log

The PLAYS, LAST PLAYED, IN LIBRARY SINCE and SKIPS cards show Music.app's numbers. The PLAYS card also shows bars for the last eight weeks and a note such as "#3 this month". Those two come from the app's own play log. Playback explains how the log counts a play or a skip.

list_weekly_plays_from_log counts the song's plays in each week. It passes over skips. It also ranks the song among the songs played in the last 30 days. is_same_song matches a logged play by catalog ID, or by name and artist. list_weekly_plays keeps the counts until the song, the length of the log or the hour changes.

The backdrop

The cover's colours fill the top of the page and the toolbar. set_backdrop keeps the cover's address at BACKDROP_PIXELS (24 pixels). draw_backdrop stretches that small image over a box 520 points high. The stretch blurs it without a blur shader. It shows only the middle band of the square cover.

The page area calls build_page_backdrop before it builds the toolbar. The backdrop thus shows behind the toolbar too. It stays in place as the page scrolls. Metal explains how the image reaches the screen.

Where the code is

  • src/app/player/player_bar.odin builds the player bar and its left end.
  • src/app/player/player_controls.odin builds the buttons, the seek bar and the volume.
  • src/app/player/panel.odin builds the side panel, its tabs and Up Next.
  • src/app/player/lyrics_panel.odin builds the Lyrics tab and holds the lead and the word timer.
  • src/app/player/lyrics_lines.odin builds each lyric line and colours its sung words.
  • src/app/player/now_playing_page.odin builds the Now Playing page, its lyric line and "More by".
  • src/app/player/now_playing_hero.odin builds the cover, the title, the pills and the buttons of the page.
  • src/app/player/now_playing_stats.odin builds the four stat cards from Music.app and the play log.
  • src/app/player/now_playing_details.odin reads Apple's answer and builds the CREDITS and SOUND cards.
  • src/app/player/now_playing_backdrop.odin draws the blurred cover behind the page.
  • src/lyrics/lyrics.odin fetches the lyrics on a thread and holds the lines of one song.
  • src/lyrics/ttml.odin reads the TTML into lines, words, romaji, translations and songwriters.
  • src/helper/helper.odin takes in the helper's posts and estimates the position.

PLAN · MILESTONE 6

Ten features, mocked up

Each feature with a mockup in the app's own style, where its data comes from, its size, and whether part of it belongs on the Now Playing page. The last section merges those parts into Stage+, the recommended Option A from the Now Playing plan grown with them. Covers, names and lyrics are from your library; play counts, dates, release days and which songs carry which quality mark are illustrative.

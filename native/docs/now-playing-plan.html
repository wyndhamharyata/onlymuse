PLAN · MILESTONE 6

Now Playing, redesigned

The current page is a spreadsheet next to a picture. This plan turns it into a stage for the song: the cover sets the mood, the numbers that matter to you (plays, last played, quality) lead, and Apple's catalog facts become credits rather than a table. It also adds the data Music.app knows and Apple's API does not.

What is wrong today

Half the page is empty

Everything sits in the top 45%. Below the cover there is nothing, so the page feels unfinished at any window size.

No hierarchy

Composer, ISRC and "Lyrics: Word by word" have the same weight. A table of ten equal rows says nothing is important.

Ignores the cover

A pink, loud cover on a flat grey page. Apple sends the cover's colours; the page uses none of them.

Repeats the player bar

Title, artist and album are already at the bottom of the window. The page adds no new answer to "what is this song to me?".

Quality is a 10-pt sticker

"LOSSLESS" is the one thing an audio app should be proud of, and it is the smallest text on the page.

Nothing to do

No favourite, no add to playlist, no copy link. It is a dead end; Back is the only way out.

Caveats: Music.app has these fields only for songs in your library; a catalog song played without adding it has no plays or date added, and the stats cards then hide. For a streamed song, Music.app may report the stream's sample rate rather than the master's; bit depth is not in the scripting dictionary, so "24-bit" comes from Apple's hi-res-lossless trait, not a measurement.

Build steps

  1. Helper: a trackInfo request: one AppleScript reading the fields above for a persistent ID, answered once per song change. Read only; the ♥ button later adds one write.
  2. Renderer: drop shadows via softness; a stretched tiny-cover backdrop with a fade into the window colour.
  3. Page: Option A's sections, each hidden when it has no data (no stats for a non-library song, no shelf without other releases).
  4. Play log: a plays table written when a song passes half its length, for "this month" and the features below.
  5. Try-it section 6c in the README; capture at 1000, 1200 and 1600 pt wide.

Ten features worth adding

Each one is mocked up, with what could merge into Stage, in features-plan.html.

  • Listening history and statsOur own play log (Music.app keeps only totals) turns into top songs, artists and albums by week, month and year, with a "your year" page.Needs the play log from step 4.
  • New from your artistsA feed of the latest releases from artists in your library, newest first. Apple Music buries this; the artist latest-release view already gives it.One request per library artist, cached daily.
  • Lyrics translation and romajiApple's lyrics answers carry ttmlLocalizations. Show the Japanese line with its romaji or English under it, toggled in the Lyrics panel.The data is already in the saved answers.
  • Favourite and rate from anywhereA ♥ in the player bar, on rows and on Now Playing, writing to Music.app's favorited, so Favourite Songs stays in sync.One AppleScript write.
  • Add to playlistFrom a row's menu or by dragging a song onto a sidebar playlist. Create a playlist inline.Apple's library playlists API.
  • Command palette (⌘K)Type to jump to any page, playlist, artist or album, or run an action: shuffle library, go to Now Playing, toggle lyrics.Reuses the search field and its IME support.
  • Quality everywhereHi-Res, Atmos and Lossless marks in every list, filters on Songs and Albums, and smart lists such as "Hi-Res in my library".Audio traits are already parsed.
  • Smarter shuffleShuffle that avoids songs played in the last few hours, songs skipped often, and two songs from one album in a row.The queue port already owns the order.
  • Mini player and menu barA small always-on-top window and a menu-bar item with the cover, controls and the current lyric line, plus global shortcuts.A second window on the same renderer.
  • Song radio and sleep timer"Play songs like this" from Apple's station for a song, and a timer that fades out and pauses at the end of a song.The song's station relationship.
